## Analytics readiness

No analytics provider, site identifier, cookie-consent approach, or data-retention policy has been approved. Therefore no third-party tracker, analytics script, cookie, or visitor-data transmission is enabled.

When an approved provider and configuration are supplied, the initial event plan should include:

- `hero_cta_click`
- `service_view`
- `industry_view`
- `work_view`
- `contact_start`
- `contact_submit`

Implementation must be reviewed against the approved privacy, cookie, consent, and legal-policy requirements before any analytics script is introduced.

# Page Structure

Each page should use the business-first messaging model in [03_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md](03_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md), shared components from [04_FRONTEND_ARCHITECTURE.md](04_FRONTEND_ARCHITECTURE.md), and the approved visual system.

| Page | Purpose and audience | Recommended sections | CTA / linking / SEO focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Orient decision-makers and create confidence. | Header; Hero; Challenges; Core Capabilities; Solutions / Industry Expertise; Featured Case Studies; Why PAL INFOSYS; Technology; Process; Testimonials; Insights; Final CTA; Footer. | Discuss Your Project; link to services, industries, work, technology, insights. Focus: enterprise software development company. |
| About | Explain who PAL INFOSYS is and how it works for prospective partners. | Positioning, principles, engineering approach, collaboration model, approved proof, CTA. | Discuss Your Project; link to process, services, contact. Focus: PAL INFOSYS software company. |
| Services | Present capabilities at overview level before future details. | Intro, service cards, engagement context, process, related industries, CTA. | Discuss Your Project; link to solutions, industries, work. Focus: custom software / enterprise application development. |

## Footer and legal pages

The footer provides primary navigation and approved contact details. Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Cookie Policy links remain unpublished until legally approved content and any required cookie behavior are supplied.

## Page rules

- Do not create empty pages solely to complete navigation; use approved static overview content.
- Services describe capabilities; Solutions describe systems and outcomes the company can enable; Industries describe domain problems and fit.
- Testimonials and work cards must follow the credibility policy. **MOCK** items are visibly marked and do not imply real clients or results.

# Project Overview

## Purpose

Create a credible corporate website for PAL INFOSYS that helps qualified organizations understand its software engineering capabilities and start a project conversation. The site must prioritize business value, clarity, and trust over technology lists.

## Positioning

PAL INFOSYS is an enterprise software and digital product engineering company. It designs, builds, modernizes, integrates, and supports software around real business operations.

## Objectives

- Explain services, solution types, industry relevance, and engineering approach.
- Generate qualified enquiries through clear, low-friction calls to action.
- Establish long-term credibility with approved work, insights, and technical expertise.
- Provide a scalable foundation for future content and detail pages.

## Target audiences

- Business owners seeking operational improvement through software.
- CTOs and technology leaders evaluating delivery partners.
- Operations heads with workflow, visibility, integration, or automation challenges.
- Organizations seeking custom software or legacy modernization.
- SaaS founders and product teams building or evolving products.

## Service areas

Custom Software Development; Enterprise Applications; SaaS Product Engineering; Application Modernization; Cloud & API Integration; Data & Database Engineering; AI & Business Automation; and Maintenance & Support.

## Industry focus

Transportation & Logistics, HR & Workforce Management, Healthcare, Finance & Accounting, Manufacturing, Retail / E-commerce, and SaaS Platforms.

## Calls to action

Primary: **Discuss Your Project**. Supporting CTAs may include **Explore Platform Expertise**, **Explore Services**, and **Talk to an Expert**, provided they route to approved relevant pages or contact.

## Initial pages

Home, About, Services, Solutions, Industries, Platform Expertise, Technology, Insights, Contact, and Sitemap. Legal policy routes remain unpublished until approved legal content is supplied. See [page structure](05_PAGE_STRUCTURE.md).

## Phase 1 scope

The approved frontend foundation: Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, React Router, design tokens, logo and approved assets, reusable components, static responsive pages, accessibility, metadata, and performance basics. The phased sequence is defined in [the roadmap](06_DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md).

## Out of scope

- Backend, database, CMS, authentication, admin tools, fake API layers, and live integrations, except the explicitly approved ASP.NET Core contact-enquiry endpoint.
- Unapproved lead handling, analytics, cookies, or third-party services.
- Claims, client material, testimonials, certifications, awards, metrics, or outcomes that are not approved.

## Content credibility rules

- No fake clients, awards, certifications, testimonials, project outcomes, or unverified statistics.
- Clearly label temporary cards, case studies, and quotes as **MOCK** until approved.
- Use evidence supplied or approved by PAL INFOSYS; omit unsupported proof rather than implying it.
- Explain the business problem and intended value before naming technologies.

## Reference policy

Netsmartz.com is permitted only as a high-level reference for enterprise information architecture and UX expectations. Do not copy its layout, language, graphics, code, animation, or brand elements. All PAL INFOSYS work must be original.
